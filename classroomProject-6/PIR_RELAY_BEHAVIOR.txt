PIR RELAY BEHAVIOR — classroomProject-6

Automatic trigger:
- PIR is sampled every 1.5 seconds.
- Three consecutive TRUE readings trigger the corresponding relay pair.
- PIR1 controls relays 1-2; PIR2 controls relays 3-4.
- Each triggered pair runs for 30 seconds.

Continuation while ON:
- During each 30-second ON interval, PIR is checked at 25.5 s, 27 s, and 28.5 s.
- If all three continuation checks are TRUE, start another full 30-second ON interval.
- The relay must NOT switch off at the end of the previous interval when continuation succeeds.

Important stop condition:
- Continuation does not make the relay permanently ON.
- If PIR later becomes FALSE, the relay remains ON only until the current 30-second interval expires, then turns OFF.
- A later new sequence of three consecutive TRUE readings can trigger another 30-second interval.
- PIR1 and PIR2 operate independently and may overlap.
